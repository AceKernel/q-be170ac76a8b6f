13.  Discuss the determinants of operating exposure.

   The main determinants of a firm’s exposure are:

     (1) The structure of the markets in which the firm sources its inputs, such as labour and

         materials, and sells its products.

     (2) The firm’s ability to mitigate the effect of exchange rate changes by adjusting its

        markets, product mix, and sourcing.
