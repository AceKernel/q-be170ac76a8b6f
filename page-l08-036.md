International Investment Vehicles #2


❑  Mutual funds that invest in foreign stocks can be grouped into several
    categories from an Australian perspective:

  ❖  Global - Investing in Australian and non-Australian shares.

  ❖  International - Investing in non-Australian shares only.

  ❖  Regional - Investing in a geographic area (e.g., Latin America; Asia).

  ❖  Country - Investing in a single country.

❑  Stock Index Futures: These are futures contracts on stock indices. Cash
     settled as the underlying (stock index) does not exist physically.

  ❖ Cash delivery avoids the transaction costs of buying and selling large number
          of stocks. Highly liquid and relatively inexpensive to get a similar position to that
          of a well-diversified portfolio.

  ❖  Speculate on/Hedge stocks with only a small cash investment. Invest in the
          entire market without bearing the risk of individual stocks.

  ❖  Institutional investors can hedge portfolios by selling index futures when they
         anticipate a decline in the market.

  ❖  Foreign investors can reduce cash movements between countries and manage
        currency risk exposure better.
