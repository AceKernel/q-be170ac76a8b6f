10. (Past Exam Question from 2024)

    You have just accepted a position as the chief investment officer at a superannuation

     fund based in Brisbane. The fund is currently underweighted in British stocks. The

     advisor hired by the Superannuation fund recommends adding 4% allocation to

      British stocks. You wish to implement the 4% exposure to British stocks immediately

    by buying equity index futures on the London Stock Exchange.

     Discuss three advantages and one disadvantage of purchasing index futures contracts

      to gain British equity exposure as an alternative to purchasing underlying securities.

     Note:  A sentence or two per advantage/disadvantage is sufficient.


1.   Cash delivery avoids the transaction costs of buying and selling large numbers of stocks.
     Highly liquid and relatively inexpensive to get a similar position to that of a well-diversified
      portfolio.
2.    Speculate on/Hedge stocks with only a small cash investment. Invest in the entire market
     without bearing the risk of individual stocks.
3.    Institutional investors can hedge portfolios by selling index futures when they anticipate a
      decline in the market.
4.    Foreign investors can reduce cash movements between countries and manage currency risk
     exposure better.


Problems: For large trades, may need to buy a huge number of contracts (due to contract
specifications)
