2.   Suppose you are a euro-based investor who just sold Microsoft shares that you had

     bought six months ago. You had invested 10,000 euros to buy Microsoft shares for $120

     per share. The exchange rate was $1.15 per euro. You sold the stock for $135 per share

    and converted the dollar proceeds into euro at the exchange rate of $1.06 per euro.

      First, determine the profit from this investment in dollar terms. Second, compute the

     rate of return on your investment in euro terms. How much of the return is due to the

     exchange rate movement?


The return in dollar terms is: (135-120)/120 = 0.125 (12.5%)

The return in Euro terms is:

              = [1+(135 – 120)/120] * [1+(0.9433 – 0.8695)/0.8695] -1

             = 1.125 * 1.085 –1 = 0.22 (22%)

The part of the return due to the exchange rate movement is:

                         (0.9433 – 0.8695)/0.8695 = 0.085 (8.5%)
