29.  Suppose during the  first half of the year, Swiss government bonds yielded a

     local-currency return of -1.6%. However, the Swiss franc rose by 8% against the

      dollar over this six-month period. Corresponding figures for France were 1.8% and

     2.6%. Which bond earned the higher U.S. dollar return? What was the return?


The dollar return on Swiss bonds equalled (1 - 0.016) × (1 + 0.08) - 1 = 6.27%.

The return on French bonds was lower at (1.018) × (1.026) - 1 = 4.45%.

In this case, Swiss franc appreciation more than offset the lower local currency return on Swiss

bonds.
