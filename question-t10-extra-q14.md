14.  Why  is  it necessary to consider real currency appreciation and depreciation

      forecasts when doing an international capital budgeting analysis?


The most important reason to consider forecasts of real currency appreciation or depreciation

is that it is likely that a change in the real exchange rate will affect the cash flows of the project.

Remember that a real depreciation of the domestic currency makes domestic exporters more
