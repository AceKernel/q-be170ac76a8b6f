The Current Account (CA)

•   It is record of a country’s trade in goods and services and of
   unilateral transfers.

•   It is divided into several sub-categories:
   ▪  Merchandise Trade: Export and import of physical goods like beef,
       cars etc.
   ▪  Services: Export and import of Services. E.g., Tourism, Education,
      Shipping and financial services etc.
   ▪  Primary Income (“investment income”): Interest income, Dividends
        etc.
   ▪  Secondary Income (“unilateral transfers”): Any transfer between
       countries that is one-way (e.g., gifts, foreign aid, pensions to retired
      people abroad, wages repatriated etc.

• CA balance = The sum of the sub-categories
            = Balance of Goods & Services + Net Primary                                                                                21
             Income + Net Secondary Income
