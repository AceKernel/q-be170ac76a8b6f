7.   Suppose Brazil starts welcoming foreign investment with open arms. How is this

      policy likely to affect the value of the real? The Brazilian current account balance?

The increased demand for Brazilian assets bought about by the new Brazilian investment policy

will cause the Real to appreciate. This will reduce the Brazilian CA balance. Another way to

address this question is to recognize that the capital inflow must be matched by a reduced

Brazilian CA balance.
