10.  Past Exam Question from 2024

Pirelli-Kim is the South Korean subsidiary of an Italian tire company. Pirelli-Kim manufactures

tires in South Korea to sell to the South Korean car manufactures such as Hyundai, SsangYong

etc. Its projected income statement is as follows:


       Sales (1,000,000 tires @ Won 120,000/tire)           120,000,000,000 won
       Direct costs (1,000,000 tires @ Won 80,000/tire)           80,000,000,000
       Fixed costs                                              4,000,000,000
       Depreciation                                           16,000,000,000
        Profit before South Korean income taxes                  20,000,000,000
      Income taxes -- none due to tax holiday                 _____________
        Profit after South Korean income taxes                    20,000,000,000
     Add back depreciation                                   16,000,000,000
      Cash flow from operations -- in won                  36,000,000,000 won

       Current exchange rate:                        Won 3,000 = €1.00

      Cash flow from operations -- in euros                     €12,000,000

Suppose that the won drops in value from 3,000/€ to 4,000/€.

What is the resulting operating exposure if
(i)     Pirelli-Kim does not raise its won sales price, but
(ii)    Pirelli-Kim's Taiwanese competitor, which had been importing tires into South Korea,
       drops out, and Pirelli-Kim's unit sales increase 10% as a result.
(iii)   Also, Pirelli-Kim’s direct and fixed costs increase by an unknown percent and this is
       given by %.

Hint: Assume that the costs increase by an unknown percent given by %.


It depends on what happens to costs! Assuming an unknown cost-increase of ( x 100)%, the
operating exposure can be found as follows:

1,200,000,000×1.1               no price increase, 10% volume increase
-80,000,000×1.1×(1+)        (×100)% cost-increase, 10% volume increase
-4,000,000×(1+)             (×100)% cost-increase
40,000,000 - 92,000,000×

Evaluated at the post-devaluation exchange rate ruble 4,000 won/€:

€10,000,000 - €23,000,000×

So, the resulting exposure is:

€12,000,000 - (€10,000,000 - €23,000,000×) = €2,000,000 + €23,000,000×

For example, at a cost-increase of 25%, the operating exposure would be:

       €2,000,000 + €23,000,000×0.25 = €7,750,000

Pirelli-Kim benefits from the fact that its competitor drops out when the won depreciates.

Pirelli-Kim could do even better if they manage to increase the won price of their tires.
