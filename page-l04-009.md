Covered Interest Arb: The Process

 Covered Interest Arbitrage can involve the following
 steps:

 ▪Borrow the domestic currency;
 ▪Exchange the domestic currency for the foreign
   currency in the spot market;
 ▪Invest the foreign currency in an interest-bearing
    instrument; and then
 ▪Sign a forward contract to “lock in” a future exchange
    rate at which to convert the foreign currency proceeds
   back to the domestic currency.




   Note: In the previous example, AUD (GBP) are the domestic (foreign) currencies.
