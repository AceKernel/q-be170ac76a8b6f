14.  Explain how exchange rate fluctuations affect the return from a foreign market,

     measured in dollar terms.

Exchange rate volatility contributes to the risk of a foreign investment through its own volatility

and in the way it covaries with local market returns. Since the covariance tends to be positive

(usually small) it is likely to add to exchange risk.
