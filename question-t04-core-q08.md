8.    Discuss the impact of transaction costs on the interest rate parity condition.

    When transaction costs are present, the Interest Rate Parity condition need no longer hold

      exactly. Deviations as large as, but not larger than, transaction costs may exist, forming

     a neutral band around the parity line.
