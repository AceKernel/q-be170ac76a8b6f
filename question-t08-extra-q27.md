27.  What characteristics of foreign securities lead to diversification benefits for Australian

     investors?


The two basic characteristics are:

(a)  Many foreign securities are issued by companies that produce goods and services not

      available from Australian companies, and these goods and services have huge global

      markets. More than half of the world’s stock market capitalisation is in non - Australian

     companies. Diversification allows access to these hugely profitable manufacturers.

(b)   All Australian companies  are more or  less  subject  to  the same  cyclical economic
