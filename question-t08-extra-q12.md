12.  Why might it be easier for an investor desiring to diversify his portfolio internationally

      to buy depository receipts rather than the actual shares of a company?

A depository receipt can be purchased on the investor’s home exchange. It is priced in the home

currency and can be purchased from a local broker. In addition, any dividends paid are converted

into local currency by the depository bank.
