4. Although  the  one-year  interest  rate  is 10%  in  the  United  States,  one-year,

   yen-denominated corporate bonds in Japan yield only 5%.

a. Does this present a riskless opportunity to raise capital at low yen interest rates?

    No. According to the international Fisher effect, the 5% interest differential reflects the

    market's expectations that the yen will appreciate by approximately 5% relative to the dollar

    over the coming year.


b. Suppose the current exchange rate is ¥140 = $1. What is the lowest future exchange rate

    at which borrowing yen would be no more expensive than borrowing U.S. dollars?

   The breakeven exchange rate is found as the solution to S = 140 x 1.05/1.10 = ¥133.64.
