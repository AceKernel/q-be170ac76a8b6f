5. Suppose that DEC buys a Swiss Franc futures contract (size is SFr $125,000) at a price

   of $0.83. If the spot rate for the Swiss Franc at the date of settlement is SFr 1 = $0.8250,

  what is DEC’s gain or loss on this contract?


DEC has bought Swiss Francs worth $0.8250 at a price of $0.83. Thus, it has lost $0.005 per

franc for a total loss of $125,000 × 0.005 = $625
