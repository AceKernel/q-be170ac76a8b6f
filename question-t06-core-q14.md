14.  Suppose your company has purchased a put option on the German mark to manage

     exchange exposure associated with an account receivable denominated in that

     currency. In this case, your company can be said to have an ‘insurance’ policy on its

      receivable. Explain in what sense this is so. *


Your company in this case knows in advance that it will receive a certain minimum dollar

amount no matter what might happen to the $/DM exchange rate. Furthermore, if the German

mark appreciates, your company will benefit from the rising mark.
