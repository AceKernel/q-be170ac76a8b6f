```text
7. What factors affect the degree of political risk faced by a firm operating in a foreign

   country?

There is some evidence to indicate that the political risk faced by the firm is related to

(1)    a function of the industry the firm operates in

(2)     the cost to the country of replacing with a state-owned operation

(3)     the health of the economy

(4)    philosophical leanings of the government

(5)     potential for civil strife in the country
```
