1.  The public corporation is owned by multitude of shareholders but managed by

    professional managers. Managers can take self-interested actions at the expense of

    shareholders. Discuss the conditions under which the so-called agency problem

     arises.

The agency problem arises when managers have control rights but insignificant cash flow

rights. This wedge between control and cash flow rights motivates managers to engage in

self-dealings at the expense of shareholders.
