Raising Capital Overseas

▪  Depositary receipts (depositary shares) are negotiable
   certificates issued by a bank to represent the underlying
   shares of stock, which are held in trust at a foreign custodian
   bank.

▪  American depository receipts (ADRs) are certificates traded in
   the United States and denominated in US dollars.

▪ ADRs are sold, registered, and transferred in the US in the
  same manner as any share of stock with each ADR
   representing some multiple of the underlying foreign share
   (allowing for ADR pricing to resemble conventional US share
   pricing between $20 and $50 per share).
