Fundamentals of BOP Accounting

•  Transactions are recorded based on double entry
  bookkeeping and, by definition, it must balance

   ▪  Every “source” must have a “use”. Every economic transaction
      recorded as a credit brings about an equal and offsetting debit
       entry.

▪ A credit (+) is an event, such as the export of a good or
   service, that records foreign exchange earned — an inflow of
   foreign exchange to the country.

   ▪  Cash inflows give rise to the demand for dollars.

▪ A debit (-) records foreign exchange spent, such as
  payments for imports or purchases of services or financial
   assets — an outflow of foreign exchange.

   ▪  Cash outflows give rise to the supply of dollars.
