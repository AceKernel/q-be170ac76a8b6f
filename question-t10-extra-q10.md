10. What is the intuition behind the NPV capital budgeting framework?


The NPV method is a discounted cash flow technique. It compares the present value of all cash

inflows associated with the proposed project versus the present value of all project outflows. If

inflows are sufficient to cover all operating and financing costs, the project adds wealth to

shareholders.
