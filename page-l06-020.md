Executing A Money Market Hedge

▪To recap, the money market hedge involves the first 3
  steps of Covered Interest Arbitrage:
            (1) Borrow, (2) Convert Spot, (3) Invest

▪The question is: Do you borrow domestic or foreign?

   – That depends on whether you’re hedging an Account Payable or
     Account Receivable (in a foreign currency).

   –  If you have a foreign receivable, you BORROW foreign.

   –  If you have a foreign payable, you INVEST foreign.

▪Note: Hedging means taking the opposite position.
  So, if you have a foreign receivable, borrow foreign.
    If you have a foreign payable, invest foreign.
