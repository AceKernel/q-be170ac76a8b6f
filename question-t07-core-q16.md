16.  In order to avoid speculation, Honda hedges only the sales it has clinched, not the

     ones it expects. Comment on Honda’s currency risk strategy.

    Honda is, in fact hedging its TRANSACTION exposure. It is hedging cash flows that

     can be viewed as being certain. However, it is leaving itself open to changes in the level

      of competition that  it might face. In other words,  it is not hedging its competitive

      exposure.
