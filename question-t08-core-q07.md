```text
For a given weight ω for the AUS portfolio, the return on the overall portfolio is:
       rP = ω × r$ + (1 – ω) × r€$.

     This implies that ω must satisfy 0.14 = ω × 0.12 + (1 – ω) × 0.2.

     Solving this equation, we get ω = 0.75 which implies that 75% of the funds are to be invested
      in the Australian portfolio, and 25% in the European portfolio.

(c)  What is the variance of the portfolio constructed using the weights determined in (b)?

     Since the variance (σP2) is the square of the volatility we have
    = (0.75)2 × 0.06 + (0.25)2 × 0.09 + 2 × 0.75 × 0.25 × 0.4 × 0.06½ × 0.09½

    = 0.0504
```
