11. Discuss what is meant by the incremental cash flows of a capital project.


Incremental cash flows are denoted by the change in total cash inflows and cash outflows that

can be traced directly to the project under analysis. Incremental cash flows can differ from the

total cash flows for a variety of reasons and these may include:

(i)    The cannibalization of a company’s products that may not add to the proposed project’s

        profits as the new product might just be taking sales from other of the company’s

       products.

(ii)   The proposed  project might  create  additional  sales  for other product  lines  (or

        subsidiaries) of the firm.

(iii)    Transfer Pricing is used by companies to manipulate the profitability of a project by

        either increasing or decreasing the price at which the project sells its output to other

        subsidiaries.
