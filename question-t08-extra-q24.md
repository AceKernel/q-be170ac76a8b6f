```text
24. The Brazilian stock market rose by 165% during 1988. Are American investors likely to

   be pleased with that performance? Explain.


These returns are stated in nominal cruzeiro terms. American investors are interested in dollar

returns. Thus, American pleasure with the Brazilian market's performance depends on how much

the cruzeiro devalued during the year. In fact, the cruzeiro (the cruzado then) devalued by about

90% during the year. Hence, the dollar return on the Brazilian market was approximately -74%.

Not good enough to keep investors happy.
```
