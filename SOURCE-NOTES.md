# Source handling and limitations

The exam-information sheet and Lecture 12 are edited public reference copies with selected non-technical text omitted. Untouched originals are retained privately. All academic content, remaining exam instructions and page numbering are retained.

- The uploaded 2026-labelled sample is byte-identical to the 2025-labelled sample and internally labelled SAMPLE2025. The 2024 actual paper has a different header/PDF but repeats the sample question family. No independent current 2026 examination is inferred.
- Tutorial 8 has core Q11 with two parts, followed by an extra Q11; Tutorials 5 and 9 restart numbering by section. IDs preserve these distinctions.
- Tutorial 10 PDF pp10-11 are an unnumbered Thorwald/BT solution fragment without the original pro forma. It is not labelled Q8 here and is not a complete numerical example.
- TSMC market data are image-based in the S2 2025 exam. The [separate data record](exam-tsmc-data.md) was visually checked. Other automatic extracts may omit figures or misorder mathematics; use the PDF before copying a source formula.
- [VaR](topic-var.md) states the tutorial's negligible-mean approximation; [international investment](topic-investing.md) flags the missing portfolio correlation; [economic exposure](topic-economic.md) defines Pirelli's cost-change parameter explicitly.
- Lecture 1 p29 has an inconsistent swap-point numerical illustration. Use the stated convention, pp25-28 and T1 Q6; original pages are unaltered.
- Tutorial 8 extra Q26's solution assumes a 5% risk-free rate absent from its stem. Do not present an unstated assumption as supplied information.
- LLSV results refer to historical data, and Lecture 11 p38 explicitly discusses a later critique. Rights, enforcement and ownership statistics are different measures; US/Mexico values in T11 Q7 are medians.
- Checked supplementary calculations are labelled as such. They are not official corrections, official exam solutions, or a guarantee of marks.
