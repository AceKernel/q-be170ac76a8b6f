Bid-Ask Prices

➢Consider the quote from a foreign currency trader:

          USDCHF 0.7770 – 0.7772*

     Base Currency                              Quote Currency

❖[USDCHF]bid = 0.7770 – the rate at which the bank (i.e.,
    trader) will buy USD (base currency) in exchange for
   CHF.
❖[USDCHF]ask = 0.7772 – the rate at which the bank (i.e.,
    trader) will sell USD (base currency) for CHF.


  Explanation: There are two currencies involved in an exchange rate pair – one is the base currency (USD in this
  example), and the other the quote currency (i.e., CHF). In this example, it is the “price of 1 USD in CHF”.

  The Bloomberg quote is the rate at which the dealer is willing to BUY USD (gives customer Swiss Francs) at the
  low rate and SELL USD (receives Swiss Francs from customer) at the high rate.

  CHF is the ISO currency code for Swiss Francs.

                                                                                9
   * Quote from Bloomberg Jan 27, 2026
