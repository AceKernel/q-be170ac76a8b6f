# Final examination scope

Source: [current information sheet](exam-information.pdf#page=1), [revision lecture](lecture-12.pdf#page=2), PDF pp2-4. These current sources govern, not old papers' cover-page rules.

| Item | Stated arrangement |
|---|---|
| Weight / marks | 50% of course assessment; 50 marks |
| Time | 2 hours plus 10 minutes perusal |
| Structure | Five multiple-choice questions (5 marks); three multipart written questions (45 marks) |
| Multiple-choice submission | Separate machine-graded answer sheet; circles in the booklet are not graded |
| Written answers | Concise answers; show calculation workings |
| Notes | One double-sided A4 handwritten sheet |
| Scope | All topics; revision topic list is explicitly non-exhaustive |

The public information sheet and revision lecture are edited reference copies. Consult the official private originals for complete permitted-material arrangements and any official updates. No individual special examination arrangements were supplied here.

Revision PDF p3 identifies Semester 2, 2025 as the lecturer's latest authored past paper and states that most examination questions are now tutorial problems. This justifies prioritising worked tutorial patterns, **not excluding topics or promising repetitions**. See the [evidence map](PATTERNS.md).

Historical sample/2024 formats and aid rules are retained solely as historical sources. The uploaded 2026-labelled sample is identical to the 2025-labelled sample and internally says SAMPLE2025. It is not a separate current examination specification.
