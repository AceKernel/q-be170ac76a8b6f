12. Discuss how the cost of capital is determined in segmented versus integrated capital

   markets.

In segmented markets, the cost-of-capital is essentially determined by the securities’ domestic

systematic risks. In integrated capital markets, on the other hand, the cost-of-capital will be

determined by the securities’ international systematic risk, regardless of nationality.
