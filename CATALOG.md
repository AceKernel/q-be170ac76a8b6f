## Selection policy

All eleven official tutorial-solution PDFs and all numbered questions are retained. The assigned LLSV paper is retained because Tutorial 11 and the revision lecture explicitly require it. Current exam information and revision govern scope. Three distinct historical/sample PDFs retain provenance; one byte-identical sample is represented by an alias, not a duplicate file.

Checked topic references and worked question routes form the primary retrieval path. Selected lecture/reading extracts are on-demand evidence. All source pages have a locator, but covers, administrative repetition, dated market snapshots, unrelated news, blank workings pages and repeated generic exposition are not duplicated into the primary text path. General textbook background is not expanded into a separate encyclopedia.

The original PDFs may contain historical arrangements, teaching typos or references omitted from the supplementary navigation. Supplementary references do not alter an original or certify an official correction. No standalone official examination solution key was supplied.
