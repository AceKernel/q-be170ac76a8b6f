# Economic exposure and exchange-rate pass-through

Keywords: operating economic real exposure cash flows competitive position price elasticity Honda Audi Pirelli Kim Intel Asahi natural hedge risk sharing.

Transaction exposure concerns contracted foreign cash flows; economic/operating exposure concerns the effect of unexpected real exchange-rate changes on future cash flows and firm value. Separate (i) operating revenues/costs/volumes from (ii) conversion into parent currency. Translation exposure is identified but not covered as a separate topic in this course.

$$OCF=(PQ-vQ-FC-\mathrm{Dep})(1-\tau)+\mathrm{Dep}.$$

Use the no-tax cash-flow form if the question has no tax. Change selling price, sales volume, imported inputs, local variable/fixed costs, depreciation/tax and currency conversion separately. Higher costs or lost demand are not captured by simply converting the original profit.

**Pass-through:** if the foreign-currency product price is fixed and $S^{D/F}$ changes, complete pass-through changes the domestic sale price by the same proportion as $S^{D/F}$. For degree $\theta$, $P_{D,1}=P_{D,0}[1+\theta(S_1^{D/F}/S_0^{D/F}-1)]$. Invert an opposite quote first.

**Honda (S2 2025 III.1), checked supplementary calculation:**

$$S_1^{\mathrm{JPY/USD}}=125\frac{1.01}{1.03}=122.5728155,$$
$$P_{\mathrm{USD},0}=4{,}000{,}000/125=32{,}000,$$
$$P_{\mathrm{USD},1}=32{,}000\left[1+0.60\left(\frac{125}{122.5728155}-1\right)\right]=\mathrm{USD}\ 32{,}380.20.$$

**Pirelli-Kim check:** baseline $CF=\mathrm{KRW}\,36\mathrm{bn}/3000=\mathrm{EUR}\,12\mathrm{m}$. With volume +10%, new rate KRW4000/EUR and cost increase $\lambda$ **as a fraction**,

$$CF_1=\frac{132\mathrm{bn}-88\mathrm{bn}(1+\lambda)-4\mathrm{bn}(1+\lambda)}{4000}
=\mathrm{EUR}\,(10-23\lambda)\mathrm{m}.$$

The loss is EUR$(2+23\lambda)$m. If $\lambda$ denotes percentage points, replace it by $\lambda/100$.

Mitigation: diversify markets/sourcing/production, adjust pricing/product mix and productivity, match currency cash flows, borrow in the revenue currency, or use risk-sharing clauses. Financial hedges alone do not remove every competitive effect. For the course's neutral-zone contract, use actual spot within the zone; beyond a boundary, average spot with that nearest boundary when risk is shared equally.
