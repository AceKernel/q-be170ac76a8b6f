Measuring Exposure: Value-at-Risk

❖  Value-at-Risk (VaR) is an approach to estimate the
    potential loss in value due to adverse exchange rate
   movements over a defined horizon with a specific
    confidence level.
❖  The intention of the VaR measure is to capture the tail
    events: the amount of portfolio loss when a 5% left-tail
    event happens over a day.

     ▪  Question managers are interested in is “how much do we stand to lose
          in market value if a 5% tail event happens to our portfolio over the next
         day?”.
     ▪   Typical confidence level is 99% or 95% which focuses on the 1% or
     5% worst case scenario.
     ▪  Regulators use Value-at-Risk (VaR) to compute capital requirements
          for financial institutions (e.g., Basel II)
