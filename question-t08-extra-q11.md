11.  As an investor, what factors would you consider before investing in the emerging stock

     market of a developing country?

There are several issues that an investor needs to be concerned with when it comes to investing in

emerging markets – the depth and liquidity of the market. The investor should look at the

concentration ratio – a measure of investment opportunities (market value of top 10 stocks/total

market capitalisation of all stocks). The lower the ratio, the larger the investment opportunities.

The market turnover ratio gives an indication of the level of liquidity of the market. The higher

the turnover ratio, the easier it is for an investor to enter and exit a market without incurring

excessive costs.
