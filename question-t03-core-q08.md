8.  Discuss the implications of the deviations from purchasing power parity for

    countries’ competitive positions in the world market.

       If the exchange rate changes satisfy PPP, competitive positions of countries will remain

      unaffected following exchange rate changes. Otherwise, exchange rate changes will

      affect the relative competitiveness of countries. If a currency appreciates (depreciates)

     by more than what is warranted by PPP, it will hurt (strengthen) the country’s competitive

      position.
