Cross-Listing of Shares

▪Cross-Listing refers to a firm having its equity shares
   listed on one or more foreign exchanges, in addition to
   the home-country exchange.
▪A firm may decide to cross-list shares for many reasons:

    •   Listing on a market with strict securities regulations and information
       disclosure can be seen as a good signal.
    •  Expands investor base for firm’s stock (improve liquidity).
    •  Establish name recognition in new capital market, paves the way to
      raise new equity.
    •  Bring stock to markets that you serve – allows customers,
      employees, suppliers, etc. to buy your stock locally. Can (a)
     compensate employees not in country of domicile; (b) pay for
      acquisitions
    •  Can mitigate possibility of hostile takeover (by increasing investor
      base).
