5. A U.S. importer will have a net cash outflow of £250,000 towards payment of ceramics

   bought from Royal Doulton, a U.K. based company. The payment date is not known

   with certainty but should occur in late August. On July 1 the importer locks in a

    ceiling purchase for foreign currency by buying PHLX call options on GBP (contract

    size is £10,000) with a strike price of U.S.$ 1.50/£ and an expiration date in September.

   The option premium on the purchase date  is U.S.$ 0.0220/£. The brokerage

   commission for purchasing/selling options is $1.50 per option contract. What is the

   most the importer will pay to buy pounds using currency options?

                                                               Past Exam Question

The thing that is unique here (relative to tutorial problem) is the brokerage fee of $1.50 per
contract.

Total cost of 25 contracts:
     = 25 × GBP10,000 × 0.0220 + 25 × 1.50

     = 5500 + 37.5 = 5537.50

The ceiling price = 5537.50/250,000 + 1.50 (exercise price) = 1.52215

N.B. 5537.50/250000 is 0.02215
