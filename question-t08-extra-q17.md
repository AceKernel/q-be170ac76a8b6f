17.  Studies show that the correlations between domestic stocks are greater than the

      correlations between domestic and foreign stocks. Explain why this is likely to be the

      case. What implications does this fact have for international investing?


Domestic stocks are more highly correlated because they are all subject in one way or another to

the state of the domestic economy. The lower correlations between domestic and foreign stocks

reflect the lower correlations between the domestic and foreign economies. These lower

correlations also imply that international investing is likely to lead to greater diversification than

just investing across industries within a country. As the text shows, these lower correlations appear

to be persisting despite the greater integration of the global economy.
