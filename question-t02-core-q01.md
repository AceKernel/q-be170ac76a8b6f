1.   In contrast to the US, Japan has realized continuous CA surpluses. What could be

     the main causes for these surpluses? Is it desirable to have continuous CA surpluses?

Japan’s continuous CA surpluses may have reflected a weak yen and high competitiveness of

Japanese industries. Massive capital exports by Japan prevented Yen from appreciating more

than it did. At the same time, foreigner’s exports to Japan were hampered by the closed nature

of Japanese markets. Continuous CA surpluses disrupt free trade by promoting protectionist

sentiment in the deficit country.
